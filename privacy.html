Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Pop Drop is made by BHDR. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What stays on your device

  • The name you enter, your scores, your records and your settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us, and deleting the game deletes them.
  • Pop Drop has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Pop Drop shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad that lets you continue a game, and now and then a full-screen ad between games.
  • To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your approximate location based on your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether Pop Drop may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. The game plays the same either way.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, a consent message from Google lets you choose how your data is used. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → PRIVACY in the game.
  • How Google uses this information is described in Google's privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

NO ADS purchase

  • The NO ADS purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

  • Pop Drop is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
