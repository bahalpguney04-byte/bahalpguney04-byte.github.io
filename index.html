How to play

Tap a group of matching balls to clear it. Clear groups one after another to build a combo. Fill three cups to earn a CHARGE, and clear six groups to earn a WIPE. The game ends only when a ball falls out of the box. HOW TO PLAY on the home screen opens a short tutorial.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Sport Drop does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game works fully offline. It does not need an account and does not connect to the internet.
  • The name you enter, your scores, your records and your settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the game deletes them.
  • The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: bahalpguney04@gmail.com.
