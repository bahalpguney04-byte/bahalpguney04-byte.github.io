How to play

Tap a group of matching items to clear it. Clear groups one after another to build a combo. Fill three icons to earn a CHARGE, and clear six groups to earn a WIPE. The game ends only when an item falls out of the box. HOW TO PLAY on the home screen opens a short tutorial.
